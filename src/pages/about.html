Why we exist

Compliance means registrations, inspections, policies, checks, training, risk assessments, contracts and certificates. It only takes one gap.

SafetyLine takes all of it on. We find what's missing, put it right and keep it right, so you can get on with running your setting, property or business.

How we work

  • Qualified people: work is carried out by suitably qualified and registered engineers and assessors.
  • Clear prices: you get a quote before anything is booked.
  • Straight answers: we tell you what's legally required and what's simply good practice.
  • Organised records: certificates are sent promptly and kept on file.
