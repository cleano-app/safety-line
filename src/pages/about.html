About SafetyLine

We make health and safety compliance simple for the people responsible for buildings.

Why we exist

Keeping a property or workplace compliant means juggling different certificates, engineers and renewal dates, and it only takes one to slip for tenants, staff or customers to be put at risk.

SafetyLine brings it all together. We organise the checks, keep track of the dates and give you one place to find every document, so you can get on with running your property or business.

How we work

  • Qualified people: work is carried out by suitably qualified and registered engineers and assessors.
  • Clear prices: you get a quote before anything is booked.
  • Straight answers: we tell you what's legally required and what's simply good practice.
  • Organised records: certificates are sent promptly and kept on file.
