About SafetyLine

A safety and compliance company. Everything to do with legal compliance and safety comes to SafetyLine.

Why we exist

Staying compliant means registrations, inspections, policies, staff checks, training, risk assessments, contracts and certificates, and it only takes one gap to put children, tenants, staff or customers at risk.

SafetyLine takes all of it on. Our experience is with schools, seminaries, nurseries and playgroups, and with larger companies, across London and the surrounding areas. We find what's missing, put it right and keep it right, so you can get on with running your setting, property or business.

How we work

  • Qualified people: work is carried out by suitably qualified and registered engineers and assessors.
  • Clear prices: you get a quote before anything is booked.
  • Straight answers: we tell you what's legally required and what's simply good practice.
  • Organised records: certificates are sent promptly and kept on file.
