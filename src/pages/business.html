Contracts

Clear contracts, fewer disputes

Health and safety needs clear contracts. When it isn't written down what a worker must and mustn't do, a company is exposed when something goes wrong.

Ask about contracts
  • What's included in the job, and what isn't.
  • Safety duties: required PPE, equipment that must be used and procedures that must be followed.
  • Responsibilities: who is responsible for what, set out clearly.
  • Signed acknowledgements of training and procedures.
