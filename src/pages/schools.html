Registration

Registering your setting

Ofsted, DfE or local authority. We prepare everything and handle the application.

Ask about registration
  • The application: Ofsted (early years and childcare), the Department for Education (independent schools) and the local authority.
  • Policies: safeguarding, health and safety, behaviour, allergies and more, written for your setting, not off the shelf.
  • Risk assessments for the premises and activities.
  • Liability insurance: we make sure the right cover is in place.
  • Staff checks: DBS checks, suitability checks and references.
Inspections

Mock inspections & inspection support

We inspect before the inspector does, and fix what we find.

Book a mock inspection
  • Mock inspection with a clear list of anything missing.
  • We fix the gaps: missing insurance, documents, records or checks.
  • Safeguarding training for staff who need it.
  • First aid training, including paediatric first aid for early years.
  • Allergy training: allergy and anaphylaxis awareness, including using adrenaline auto-injectors.
  • Guidance through the inspection: we can walk you through it together.
Schools & seminaries

School & seminary compliance

Safer recruitment, records and health & safety, in line with Keeping Children Safe in Education.

Ask about school compliance
  • Safer recruitment and vetting of new staff.
  • Single Central Record (SCR): set up, review and keep up to date.
  • Attendance and admissions procedures and records.
  • Risk assessments, including educational visits.
  • Fire safety and evacuation arrangements.
  • Gas safety and kitchen safety.
  • Manual handling and lone working.
  • First aid arrangements, plus accident and incident records.
  • Site inspections and hazard reporting, including playground and PE equipment checks.
  • Contractor and building works safety.
